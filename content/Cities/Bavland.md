**Bavland** is a small mining region in the nation of Jah Keved. The people from Bavland are called either Bav or Bavlanders. 

The party started their adventure in Bavland, where they were kidnapped by the [[Envisagers]]. The party escaped, found [[Leif]] researching ruins around the Envisagers Bavland hideout, and helped Leif resuce his wife, [[Elara]].

After resting for a few days, the party then left Bavland to head to [[Silnasen]] to research some potential information about imprisoned parshmen.
