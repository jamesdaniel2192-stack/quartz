
Kharbranth, the City of Bells, is an independent city-state on Roshar. It is one of the five Vorin nations and home to the Palanaeum, the largest library on Roshar. 

Kharbranth is known for the quality of its hospitals, and people come from all over the world to study medicine from Kharbranth's surgeons.
