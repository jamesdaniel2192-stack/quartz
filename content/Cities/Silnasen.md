The party hasn't had a lot of time in Sinanse; they spent less than a day here. They found a warehouse full of dead parshmen and all of the corpses had tubes coming out of their arms. 

[[Ravan]] took the deaths of these parshmen pretty hard as he was the one who rescued them and put them in [[Dahl]]'s care. The party didn't find any notes or documents in the warehouse (except for a few death rattles that were written on blackboards), and as they were investigating the rest of the warehouse, the party was attacked by [[Cremling Swarm]]s. 

They rebuffed the swarms, but the cremlings did knock over a kind of bomb fabrial that set the warehouse on fire. The party was able to get out unscathed, but they did have to book it out of town. 

It should be noted that as the party was fleeing Silnasen, [[Nucko the Button Thief]] decided that he was hungry and decided to stop by a chouta vendor. Nucko was able to a free chouta by annoying the bejesus out of the shopkeeper. He was able able to get the chouta and get out of Silnansen before any guards found him.
