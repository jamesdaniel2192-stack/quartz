
[[Theren]]'s boat which he stole from a firemoss merchant. That firemoss merchant worked for [[Captain Sebarial]] and Theren stealing the Wind's Favor is really what solidified captain Sebarial's desire to be friends with the party. 

It's maiden voyage took the party from [[Vedenar]] to [[Kharbranth]] where the party was able to narrowly sail out of a highstorm and land in Kharbranth. As the ship arrived in Kharbranth, it came in a little hot and crashed into the docks. 

Theren was able to negotiate decent docking fees and ship maintainence costs and was promised the ship would be repaired in about a months' time. 

After three weeks [[Jamma]] informed the party that the Wind's favor had been repaired and was ready to sail once again.
