Dblar was a sailor on [[Captain Sebarial]]'s ship. He isn't the brightest or strongest, but he actually has a talent for gambling and games of chance. Unfortunately, Sebarial used underhanded tactics to win every gambling game against Dblar and Dblar quickly racked up a very high gambling debt to Sebarial. 

The party met Dblar when he invited them to participate in cremling races! [[Ravan]] made a deal with Sebarial to wipe out his debts in exchange for a favor. Dblar now serves as the party's guide while in Thaylenah. 

While the party was dealing with the fallout of the events in [[Klna]], Ravan tasked Dblar with sailing the party's ship up north near where at the temple where the perfect gemstone that [[Gavlin]] was tasked with obtaining is located.
