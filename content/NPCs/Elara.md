**Background**

Elara is the wife of the innkeeper and scholar, [[Leif]], in [[Bavland]] She is a compassionate and resourceful apprentice artifabrian with a bright, curious mind. Her disappearance during a fieldwork trip becomes the catalyst for the party's first major quest.

Elara is an apprentice artifabrian in her early 30s. She is described as having striking blue eyes and golden-brown hair, and carries herself with an elegant grace . 

As an apprentice, Elara's mind is constantly at work, analyzing, questioning, and experimenting. Her passion for her craft is evident, but her curiosity sometimes outweighs her caution. 

**Campaign Notes**

The party first met Elara in Bavland, where they had to rescue her from being cornered by a [[WhiteSpine]]. The party ultimately saved her, and she helped [[Gavlin]] craft some good stuff for the party. 

After the party left Bavland, they haven't seen Elara since - they've just heard from Leif that she is 
"doing well". 

It is unknown if Elara knows her husband's past and his association with the knights radiant.
