### **Background**

Elias was a miner and a loving father to Kael, but his life was irrevocably shattered during an attack by a group of captors led by a man wielding a Shardblade . In a desperate act to protect his son, Elias was gravely injured but, unbeknownst to Kael, survived the massacre . He spent years recovering, consumed by a searing rage and a desperate need for the power to never be helpless again. This obsession led him to the [[Envisagers]] a cult that believes power can be forged through suffering.

Elias rose swiftly through the cult's ranks, not through blind faith, but through his terrifying conviction born from personal trauma. He became the Head Priest of his branch, commanding the cult's operations and escalating their "trials" to new, more brutal extremes. He is driven by a twisted ideology that equates absolute control with "true freedom," believing he is a savior who forces others to overcome the weaknesses he believes are inherent to human nature. He now wields his brutal philosophy as a tool to pursue a grim form of justice.

Sometime during his rise to power in the Envsiagers, Elias found his wife, [[Mirad]]. While she was not being held against her will, she was likely severely manipulated by Elias by the two of them planning out how to find their son. Additionally, he also was able to find and kidnap [[Tann]], the individual who kidnapped Kael. 

Elias planned with [[Shai]], his second-in-command to kidnap Kael and bring him to Kharbranth. However, he  and the party escaped the Envisager's hideout in [[Bavland]], and so the two of them plotted to get him to them in Kharbranth. [[Jarro]], was a member of the Envisagers and he agreed to spy on the party to help Shai and Elias to get Kael back to them. 

Eventually Kael found Elias, and Elias tried to get Kael to join the Envisagers. He was friendly, repentant, and charming at first. He told Kael that he had failed as a father, and his sole purpose since losing him was to accumulate power to avoid something like this happening ever again. 

When Kael refused to join up, Elias brought out a Willshaper's worst nightmare. Elias brought out Tann in a cage, and Kael's mother. He told Kael that he had to choose: Save his mother from death, or saving Tann from imprisonment. He had to choose between imprisonment or his radiant oaths. 

Kael was able to save his mother, but in his attempts to also save Tann from his imprisonment, Elias threw the shardblade that he took from Tann at Tann himself and killed Tann. 

Elias and Kael continued to fight, and right before Elias was able to strike a devastating blow, Jaro betrayed Elias, swore the first ideal of the knights radiant, and helped Kael kill his father. 

Elias was buried under the stones of his Envisager hideout by his son, along with his shardblade.
