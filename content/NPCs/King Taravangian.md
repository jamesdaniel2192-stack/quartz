**Campaign Notes**

The party has interacted with Taravangian on several occasions: 

1. Taravangian accompanied [[Rilano]] to a imprisonment area where [[Ravan]] was being held. He approved of Ravan having his blood drained to try and trigger a death rattle. 
2. Taravangian met with Rilano and [[Gavlin]] in [[Gavlin's Lab]] where he approved of Rilano trying to recruit Gavlin to [[The Diagram]].
3. The party is working on a heist to steal from Taravangian.
4. Taravangian knew of the heist on his palace because [[The Muffin Man]] tipped him off. He set several traps that failed and ran away when Ravan tried to attack him. 
	1. At one point it should be noted that [[Theren]] was able to grapple the king, but the king successfully passed a strength test and threw him off. 
5. Taravangian set up traps in an attempt to get leverage with the party to try and recruit Gavlin and to get him to fully commit to the Diagram.
