Shai is a dustbringer radiant and a member of the [[Envisagers]]. She was an apprentice of [[Elias]] and a high ranking member of the Envisagers. Not much is known of her background, but she is passionate about this organization and accomplishing their goals. 

**Campaign Notes**

Shai was the party's first boss fight and they fought her and other Envisager members. She was fighting Kael in a solo duel and at one point [[Kael]] trapped her using his cohesion surge. She ripped her feet out of the stone, healed, and then glided away into a highstorm. 

Kael had an interaction with her at the [[Bavland]] [[Glyphpool]], where she told Kael the Envisagers had her mother, but the party hasn't interacted with much with her since.
