Stukov was the city-lord of [[Klna]]. He is a light-eyes of the sixth Dahn and was in the process of raising money to start a dueling league in Klna and Thaylenah. He robbed the dark-eyes of Klna to auction off their rare goods to fund this league, and if he hadn't tried to rob [[Prawf]] and her perfect gemstone, it's entirely possible he would have gotten away with this theft. 

The dark-eyes (influenced by Prawf) eventually rebelled and overthrew the lighteyes of Klna.
