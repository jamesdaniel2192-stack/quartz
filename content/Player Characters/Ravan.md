### Backstory

> [!HELP]- Amonrava's Backstory
> Amonrava was born to an insignificant Listener tribe on the Shattered Plains in the year 1155. Their first few years of life are not well-remembered, but sometime during 1159, Amonrava’s tribal family were raided and enslaved by bandits working for an Alethi noble family claiming to be relatives of the Kohlins. Prior to manifesting Mediationform, they experienced episodes of heightened cognition and observation, but remaining silent out of fear of retribution.
> 
> Prior to the death of Evi Kohlin in 1163 and coinciding with Rira’s disdain for house Kohlin’s theft of their only shardplate, a noble Riran family purchased Amonrava and the remaining Listener slaves from the “Kohlin” relatives. (This was done with the intent to weaken House Kohlin, but was later acknowledged by the house that spite and pride foolishly and humorously clouded their judgment in making the purchase.) Under the much more kind rule of Rira’s House Renmurri, Amonrava felt at ease and confident enough to speak openly to the other serving staff. The Master Servant of House Renmurri, Dahl, was stunned to meet a Parshman who asked so many questions about their faith, diplomacy, and clerical duties. Dahl, feeling bound by the tenets of Vorinism and his belief in the One, took Amonrava on as an understudy, teaching him how to maintain a noble house and bringing him along as a “slave” to listen in and observe his dealings with nobles and merchants. (This is when the name of Ravan was first given, as a means of protecting Amonrava’s identity as a “uniquely intelligent Parshman.”)
> 
> After serving alongside Dahl for 7 years, Amonrava was granted clemency by House Renmurri after citing the Iri belief that he was entitled to travel Roshar to prepare his people for the Next World and that he would gather as many as he could in Rira for this purpose.
> 
> Amonrava’s mission is greatly influenced by the Riran’s kindness, and he aims to liberate the oppressed and create a safe-haven for the Listeners on the Babatharnam border. Mediationform has only come recently, but the smooth features and clarity of mind granted by the form feel natural to Amonrava; as such, Mediationform has become Amonrava’s preferred form to travel and communicate with. At the start of the campaign, Amonrava is thrown into prison after an unsuccessful appeal for the freedom of some Parshmen slaves, being called a swindler and freak among Parshmen. He does not speak to his relationship with House Renmurri often, in order to protect both himself and his patron house.
