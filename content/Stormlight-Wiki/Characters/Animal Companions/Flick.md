# Flick

**Status:** Alive
**Bonded to:** [[Torin]]

## Overview
Small, sneaky, and criminally underestimated. Flick is Torin's ferret, and this wiki would like the record to show that a ferret notched the campaign's first confirmed kill before most of the party had drawn a weapon in anger. He's also a professional-grade pickpocket, which makes him strictly more useful to have around than several of the humans on this roster.

## Key Moments
- Rushed down [[Shield]]'s flank during the meteorite ambush and bit a soldier's nose clean off for a 1-HP finishing blow — the campaign's first confirmed kill, and it belongs to the ferret.
- Regularly deployed as a four-legged pickpocket during Torin's undercover work, lifting marks and pamphlets off Shifty, a guard's belt pouch, and whatever else wasn't nailed down.
- Slipped into pockets unnoticed while Torin ran cover identities like "Joran" — the kind of quiet competence nobody claps for.
- Quietly the true culprit behind the Gold Tooth Incident: while Nucko sweated through a full interrogation as the prime suspect, it was Flick who'd lifted Sebarial's mother's gold tooth and dropped it in the ocean. Nucko took the heat. Flick has never once been implicated. Justice, allegedly, exists.
