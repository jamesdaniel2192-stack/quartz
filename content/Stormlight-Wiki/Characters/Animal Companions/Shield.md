# Shield

**Status:** Alive
**Bonded to:** [[Torin]]
**Breed:** Ryshadium

## Overview
Torin's Ryshadium warhorse, and a certified Very Good Boy in every sense the term allows. Ryshadium famously don't take riders they haven't chosen — so the fact that Torin never once had to roll for control says everything about the arrangement. This is not a horse. This is a partner with hooves.

## Key Moments
- Helped Kael lay traps faster ahead of the Kilna siege — logistics support, on request, no complaints.
- Charged and trampled burning enemy soldiers during the meteorite ambush, which, delightfully, doubled as putting out Nucko's fire in the process. Unintentional heroism, twice in one move.
- Targeted by enemy soldiers wielding sticky gloves trying to hijack him mid-battle — an actual attempted horse-napping — foiled when [[Flick]] intervened. Ferret and Ryshadium, watching each other's backs.
