# Chungledown Bimm

**Also known as:** Chungledown Bim
**Status:** Alive
**Role:** Sebarial's first mate

## Overview
A name that started as a running joke and somehow became a real person. Tink was convinced his rival was named "Chungledown Bim" and killed [[Marren]] on that mistaken belief — and then, against all odds, an actual Chungledown Bimm turned out to exist, serving as Sebarial's real first mate. Described in the record as having a beard like "nine separate mustaches," complete with bits of teeth in it, which this wiki has chosen not to investigate further. Bimm has spent the campaign getting thrown into oceans, falling off ropes, and diving in front of blows meant for other people — a man built almost entirely out of collateral damage and loyalty.

## Key Moments
- Thrown into the ocean by [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]]'s Gravitational Slam within moments of meeting him, then fished back out by Ravan on Sebarial's orders. A baptism, of sorts.
- Attempted to swing over on a rope during the ship boarding fight, slipped immediately, and had to be rescued by [[Gavlin]]. Nautical competence: pending.
- Dove to take a hit meant for the unconscious [[Kael]] during the final battle against Sebarial — the closest thing to a genuinely selfless moment this wiki has on file for him.
