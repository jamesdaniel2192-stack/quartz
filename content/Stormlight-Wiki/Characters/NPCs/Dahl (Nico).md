# Dahl / Nico

**Status:** Alive
**Affiliation:** [[The Diagram]]

## Overview
Ravan's former mentor and patron, who "freed him from captivity" and to whom Ravan felt a deep debt — before Dahl turned on him, drained his Stormlight, and had him arrested and imprisoned in Kharbranth.

## Key Moments
- Believed dead for a time; reappeared alive at a New Natan tavern.
- Has a twin brother, **Nico**, a servant of House Renmuri — it was later deduced that Dahl has been impersonating Nico to gain political favor for the Diagram.
- Interrogated [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]] personally using a philosophical utilitarian framing ("would you sacrifice 100 to save 90%?").
