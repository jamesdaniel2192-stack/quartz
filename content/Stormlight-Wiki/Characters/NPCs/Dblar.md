# Dblar

**Status:** Alive
**Role:** First mate turned shopkeeper, [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)|The Glyph Pool]]

## Overview
A pirate whose incompetence is so consistent it's basically a magic system of its own — and somehow the party loves him for it. He's been along since the day they first stole a boat, has a pet hog named Dog, once crashed a ship directly into a smuggling operation (an outcome so lucky it earned the party an actual Letter of Marque from the Thaylen Navy), and — against every odd the table could generate — landed the killing blow on the Yu-Nerig by throwing a rapier at it. Nobody, least of all Dblar, has fully explained how any of that worked.

## Key Moments
- Earned the Thaylen Navy's Letter of Marque off the back of his smuggler skirmish. [[Nucko]] stole it at some point, because obviously he did, then gave it back — professional courtesy among people who steal things for a living.
- Runs the front counter of the party's own storefront now, [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)]] — pirate to proprietor, a career pivot nobody saw coming.
- Adopted an unhatched egg after a chicken-buying trip went sideways in a way only Dblar's trips ever do. [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]] is currently on incubation duty.
