# Dblar

**Status:** Alive
**Role:** First mate turned shopkeeper for [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)|The Glyph Pool]]

## Overview
A beloved, endearingly incompetent pirate who's been a fixture since the party first stole a boat. Owns a hog named Dog, crashed a ship into smugglers (which incidentally got the party a Letter of Marque from the Thaylen Navy), and landed the killing blow on the Yu-Nerig with a thrown rapier.

## Key Moments
- Received the Thaylen Navy Letter of Marque after his smuggler skirmish — later briefly stolen by [[Nucko]], then returned.
- Now runs the front counter of the party's storefront, [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)]].
- Adopted an unhatched egg (currently being kept warm by [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]]) after a mix-up buying a replacement "chicken."
