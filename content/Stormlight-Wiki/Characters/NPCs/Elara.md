# Elara

**Status:** Alive
**Role:** Amateur artifabrian, wife of [[Leif]]

## Overview
Went missing near Bavland while researching spren, which kicked off one of the party's earliest side quests. Found cornered by a whitespine amid a cloud of red-violet fearspren — which, as it turned out, was less an unlucky coincidence and more the plan working exactly as designed.

## Key Moments
- Rescued by the party from a whitespine in the campaign's early sessions — one of the first times the party played hero on purpose.
- Revealed, after the fact, that she'd been deliberately trying to attract fearspren for her Fabrial research. The near-death experience wasn't a research setback; it was the research. Dedication to the craft, or a mild disregard for her own survival — this wiki declines to pick a side.
