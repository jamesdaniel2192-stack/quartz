# Elias

**Status:** Last seen alive at the Envisager ritual site
**Family:** Father of [[Kael]] and [[Jaro]]

## Overview
Believed dead for most of the campaign, Elias turned out to be alive and the architect of the Envisager ritual site. Confronted Kael with an impossible choice — free Tan (Kael's circus abuser) or save Kael's mother, but not both — the moment that pushed Kael to swear his Second Ideal.
