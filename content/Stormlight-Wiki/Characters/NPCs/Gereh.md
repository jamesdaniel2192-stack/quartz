# Gereh

**Status:** Retired
**Origin:** Scadrial

## Overview
Gereh is the party's off-world encyclopedia — a Scadrian expat who showed up, dispensed a career's worth of Cosmere lore, and had the good sense to retire rather than get dragged into whatever the party was about to do next. Confirmed that [[Sebarial]] hailed from Nalthis (relevant background, given what Sebarial eventually turned out to be, and given that he's now dead), and warned [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]] that walking off Roshar entirely would mean breaking his Skybreaker oaths — a warning delivered with the weary patience of a man who's had this conversation before.

## Key Moments
- Revealed that [[Dahl (Nico)|Dahl]] has a twin brother, Nico — the loose thread that let the party unravel Dahl's whole House Renmuri impersonation scheme.
