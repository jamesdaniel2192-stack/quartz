# Gereh

**Status:** Retired
**Origin:** Scadrial

## Overview
An off-worlder from Scadrial who provided major Cosmere lore to the party, including confirming Sebarial hails from Nalthis, and warning Ravan that leaving Roshar would be nearly impossible without breaking his Skybreaker oaths.

## Key Moments
- Revealed Dahl has a twin brother, Nico — leading the party to deduce Dahl has been impersonating Nico within House Renmuri.
