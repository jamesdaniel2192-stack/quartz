# Ishren Son Kez

**Status:** Unknown, last seen looming over a grappled [[Kael]] like a man who'd just won an argument he started
**Origin:** Shin, a truthless — which, for the unfamiliar, is the Shin equivalent of "guy with a black mark on his soul and a chip on his shoulder to match"

## Overview
Ishren crashed a perfectly good gala rescue with the kind of reveal that ruins everyone's night: the imprisoned people [[Kael]] had been so nobly trying to save were bait, a distraction dangled specifically to keep him away from his friends. Rude. Effective, but rude. He also claimed to answer to several different masters who rotate nightly, which is either a genuinely unsettling employment arrangement or the single best excuse for missing a shift ever devised. This wiki doesn't know which and isn't in a hurry to find out.

## Key Moments
- Squared off with Kael one-on-one and took real damage for it — Kael hit hard enough to daze him, which is more than most people who cross a truthless get to say.
- Turned the whole fight around with a single, devastatingly well-placed punch to a sensitive spot, stunning Kael and closing out the session with him pinned in a grapple. Ishren won the scene. History, as usual, declined to say what happened next.
