# Jama

**Status:** Alive, currently re-hired as deckhand (subject to change, per usual)
**Family:** Cousin of [[Nucko]]

## Overview
A Herdazian hired hand whose employment history with Nucko reads like a soap opera with a payroll dispute at its center — quit, fired, rehired, quit again, on a loop tight enough to set a watch by. The two most recently patched things up in the aftermath of the meteorite ambush, for whatever that reconciliation is worth now that Nucko's walked off to go dismantle the Ghostbloods from the inside. Family, it turns out, outlasts even Nucko's business relationships.
