# Jaro

**Status:** Alive, currently allied with [[Sebarial]]
**Affiliation:** Formerly [[The Envisagers]]; later independent
**Family:** Brother of [[Kael]]

## Overview
First found imprisoned in Bavland alongside Kael's mother's captors, Jaro joined the party early as a lovable, chaos-prone ally (naked walking arc, sinkhole enthusiast) — before being revealed as an embedded Envisager agent who had been tracking Kael the entire time.

## Key Moments
- Revealed as [[Kael]]'s brother and confessed to spying for the Envisagers throughout the party's journey.
- Owns a Shardblade; briefly held Theren's family Shardblade after his death before the party let him keep it.
- Left the party (2/19/26) admitting he couldn't "reach his potential" with the group; gave heartfelt parting gifts to everyone.
- Later reappeared working directly with [[Sebarial]], using Stormlight to open a transit portal for Gavlin and Rilano.
