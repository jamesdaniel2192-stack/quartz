# Jaro

**Status:** Alive, last aligned with [[Sebarial]] — before Sebarial ended up dead, so draw your own conclusions about how that partnership wrapped up
**Affiliation:** Formerly [[The Envisagers]]; later independent, later still Sebarial's problem
**Family:** Brother of [[Kael]] *(deceased)*

## Overview
Jaro's introduction to this campaign was pure comic relief: found imprisoned in Bavland alongside the people holding Kael's mother, he tagged along as a lovable disaster — there was a naked-walking arc, there was enthusiastic sinkhole appreciation — right up until the reveal that he'd been an embedded Envisager agent tracking Kael the entire time. Turns out the chaos was a very good cover story.

## Key Moments
- Revealed as [[Kael]]'s brother, and confessed to spying for the Envisagers for the whole of the party's journey up to that point.
- Owns a Shardblade of his own; briefly held Theren's family blade after Theren's death before the party let him keep custody of it.
- Left the party (2/19/26), citing an inability to reach his potential while tagging along — handed out genuinely heartfelt parting gifts on the way out, which is more grace than most people manage on their way to working for a demigod.
- Resurfaced working directly for [[Sebarial]], using Stormlight to crack open a transit portal for Gavlin and Rilano. His current employment status is, as of Sebarial's death, presumably in flux.
