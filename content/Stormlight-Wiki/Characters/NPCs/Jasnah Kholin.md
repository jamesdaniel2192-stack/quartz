# Jasnah Kholin

**Status:** Alive
**Ward:** Shallan (redheaded)

## Overview
Yes, that Jasnah Kholin. She showed up in a Kharbranth alleyway, minding her own business, ward in tow, when four thugs made the catastrophic decision to pick a fight with her in front of [[Nucko]] and [[Theren]]. The wiki does not know what those four men were thinking. The wiki suspects they weren't.

## Key Moments
- Ended the confrontation before it could reasonably be called one: one thug became a screaming pillar of flame, one became stone, one became crystal, and Jasnah walked away without so much as adjusting her sleeve. Nucko and Theren watched an Elsecaller casually redecorate three grown men and, wisely, did not intervene.
