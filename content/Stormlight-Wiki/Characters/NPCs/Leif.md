# Leif

**Status:** Alive, memory altered
**Location:** Formerly Bavland, later Kharbranth (Glyphpool tavern)

## Overview
Tavern owner whose wife Elara (an amateur artifabrian) went missing researching spren, kicking off the party's earliest side quest. Reappeared later in Kharbranth with noticeably altered memory, but able to sense that several party members had bonded spren.
