# Liss

**Status:** Alive
**Role:** Clerical worker, Kholin War Camps land registry

## Overview
The unsung hero of party logistics: a couple of well-timed bribes from Liss got the party's storefront plot registered inside the Kholin Princetom, no questions asked — the kind of quiet competence that never makes the highlight reel but keeps the whole operation running. She was also, for a while, the center of a genuine love triangle, briefly entangled with both [[Torin]] and [[Kael]] before settling on officially courting Torin. Kael's death has since closed that particular chapter for good, leaving Torin as the one she actually chose.
