# Luna

**Status:** Alive
**Role:** Hired mercenary muscle for the party

## Overview
A horneater warrior first met as a battlefield ally during the war for Vedenar, Luna reentered the party's orbit later via a chance reunion at a gambling ring in the Kholin War Camps — and [[Kael]] promptly recruited her as both a training partner and permanent hired muscle. She's stayed on ever since: muscle for hire, still on the clock long after the man who hired her.
