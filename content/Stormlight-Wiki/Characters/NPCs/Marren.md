# Highprince Marren (Merren Vethar)

**Status:** Deceased
**Relationship:** Theren's boyhood rival

## Overview
Murdered Theren's father and stole the family Shardblade. Confronted and taunted Theren at the Vedenar docks before fleeing when Nucko's illusion made him believe Theren's father had returned from the dead.

## Key Moments
- Killed in a later battle by Tink, who mistook him for the mythical "Chungledown Bim" and slit his throat, claiming his Shardblade.
- [[Theren]], enraged by the Thrill, then killed Tink and reclaimed the family blade.
