# Mraize

**Status:** Alive. Still collecting.
**Affiliation:** Local leader, [[The Ghostbloods]] — the man at the actual top of the org chart Nucko spent his whole life inside without knowing it.

## Overview
Mraize is what happens when "eccentric collector" and "runs an intercontinental spy cult" turn out to be the same job description. The Ghostbloods' grand leader on Roshar, he keeps a personal trophy case of off-world oddities — reportedly including *The Book of the Returned* — and, per at least one very pointed warning passed along secondhand, will have you executed for touching the display case. Charming. When Nucko's entire double-agent history came spilling out to the party, Mraize's response wasn't panic or damage control; it was a business proposal. Give the man credit for consistency of character even when his organization's cover is blown wide open.

## Key Moments
- Calmly confirmed to Nucko that he already knew about Kel's disappearance and Jernah's confession — because of course he did. This man collects secrets the way other people collect buttons.
- Struck a deal to "make amends": let Nucko forge a duplicate of *The Book of the Returned*, seeded with falsified intel about Odium's Perpendicularity, specifically to send [[Sebarial]] chasing his tail through the mountains of Shinovar instead of the real site on the Shattered Plains. Misdirecting a god-killing demigod is apparently just Tuesday for this man.
- Promoted Nucko within the organization for services rendered and promised help tracking down the last two Ghostbloods responsible for his parents' murder — which, depending on how cynical you're feeling, is either genuine loyalty or the world's most patient long con. This wiki isn't taking a side.
