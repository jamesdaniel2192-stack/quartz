# Mraize

**Status:** Alive
**Affiliation:** Local leader, [[The Ghostbloods]]

## Overview
The Ghostbloods' grand leader on Roshar, a collector of off-world oddities including *The Book of the Returned*. After Nucko's true identity came to light, Mraize sought to "make amends" and struck a deal.

## Key Moments
- Confirmed to Nucko that he knew of Kel's disappearance and Jernah's confession.
- Agreed to let Nucko forge a duplicate of *The Book of the Returned*, with falsified information about Odium's Perpendicularity, to mislead [[Sebarial]].
- Promoted Nucko and promised to help track down the last two of his parents' killers.
