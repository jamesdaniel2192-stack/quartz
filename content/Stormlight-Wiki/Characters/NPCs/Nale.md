# Nale

**Status:** Not seen recently. Which, given how this campaign works, means absolutely nothing.
**Role:** Azish Vizier, professional intimidator, apparent button connoisseur against his will.

## Overview
Nale showed up early, asked exactly the right unsettling questions, and left the entire party rattled in a way that few NPCs since have managed without a Shardblade involved. He interrogated the group cold and precise, wanted to know if Ravan had noticed "any odd spren following you around" (deeply, correctly suspicious), and informed Kael — to his face — that he'd kill him for his past wrongdoings if he only had the legal jurisdiction to do it. Even Leif, a tavernkeeper who has presumably seen some things, pulled Ravan aside afterward just to warn him that Nale is dangerous and has been quietly accumulating authority for a very long time. Naturally, Nucko's response to all of this menace was to try to pickpocket one of Nale's buttons mid-interrogation. Nale caught his wrist, dealt actual damage, and delivered a warning that he would kill him if he tried it again. This did not deter Nucko even slightly, as a pattern of behavior across this entire wiki will confirm.

Deputized the party on the spot — hunt down the Envisagers, get paid in wealth and power — and handed over a map narrowing the search to Duma Dari, Kharbranth, or Yulay.

## Key Moments
- Interrogated the party at Leif's tavern with the bedside manner of a man who has personally ended several conversations permanently.
- Caught Nucko attempting to steal a button off him in real time. Grabbed his wrist, dealt 4 impact damage, and promised worse next time. Nucko has stolen from gods, demigods, and museums since. He never tried Nale again.
- Deputized the party to hunt the Envisagers, offering wealth and power for their heads.
- In the one moment of levity this man has ever produced, Ravan talked him into a wager via a genuine natural 20 Persuasion check: if the party wiped out the Envisagers, Nale would hand over every single button on his coat to Nucko. This wiki does not know if that debt was ever collected. This wiki suspects it should follow up.
