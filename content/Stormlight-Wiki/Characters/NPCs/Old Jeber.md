# Old Jeber

**Also known as:** Old Jebber
**Status:** Unknown

## Overview
One of three separate men named some arrangement of "Jeber" who all happened to be standing around the same cremling races at the Kilna alcove stopover when [[Nucko]] decided the crowd was less a spectator event and more a personal shopping trip. This wiki would like it on record that it did not choose this naming convention and has no idea who did. Old Jeber's specific contribution to the historical record: having a pair of ladies' underwear removed from his pocket by Nucko, mid-heist, for reasons that remain — and likely will always remain — entirely unexplained. Not by Nucko. Not by the underwear. Not by anyone.

## Key Moments
- Got pickpocketed by Nucko during the cremling races while the rest of the party was busy betting chips on rodents with legs. Nucko's haul from Old Jeber's pocket was a pair of ladies' underwear, provenance unknown, purpose unknown, dignity of all parties involved: also unknown.
