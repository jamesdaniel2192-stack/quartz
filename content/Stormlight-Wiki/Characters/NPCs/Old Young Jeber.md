# Old Young Jeber

**Also known as:** Old Young Jebber
**Status:** Unknown

## Overview
Not to be confused with [[Old Jeber]] or [[Young Jeber]], both of whom were also present, also got pickpocketed, and are also apparently unrelated enough in naming logic that this wiki has simply given up trying to sort out the family tree. Old Young Jeber's claim to fame is being the third leg of Nucko's cremling-race crime spree — the one who lost a sphere for the crime of standing near a professional thief with nothing better to do that afternoon.

## Key Moments
- Had a sphere lifted straight off his person by [[Nucko]] during the cremling races, who then — purely to see if he could — immediately tried to pass it off to a completely different, unsuspecting bystander. Whether that handoff worked is not recorded. This wiki assumes it did, because it usually does.
