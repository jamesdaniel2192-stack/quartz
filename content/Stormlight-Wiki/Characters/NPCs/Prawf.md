# Prawf

**Status:** Alive, allied with the party (and enthusiastically so, once she stopped trying to kill them)
**Role:** Thalen priestess

## Overview
Guardian of an honest-to-Stormfather seaweed-and-barnacle-encrusted temple somewhere north of [[Kilna]], sitting on a flawless ruby gemstone and apparently unbothered by the smell. Tests visitors with a spren-triggering maze and something called the Mirror of Passions, which — despite the name — is less "romantic trial" and more "say something true about yourself or die," a distinction the party learned the hard way.

## Key Moments
- Flunked the party on a Glory Spren door check, declared them "deceivers" on the spot, and responded the only reasonable way a temple guardian can: four White Spines, no warnings, no do-overs.
- Somehow forgave all of that the moment the party actually passed the Mirror of Passions with real honesty — handed over the ruby without further theatrics and pledged herself a permanent ally. Fastest turnaround this wiki has on record from "almost got mauled to death" to "we're friends now."
