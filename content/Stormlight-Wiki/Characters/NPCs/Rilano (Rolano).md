# Rilano (Rolano)

**Status:** Alive, fugitive
**Role:** [[Gavlin]]'s mentor and fellow artifabrian

Reunited with Gavlin in Kharbranth, secretly a Diagram agent at the time. Later abandoned the Diagram entirely after they ordered a hunt on him, and now works openly alongside Gavlin in [[Sebarial]]'s lab, [[The Shallow Crypts]].
