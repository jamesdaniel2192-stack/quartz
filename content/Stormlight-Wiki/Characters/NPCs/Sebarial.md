# Captain Sebarial

**Status:** Deceased — cognitive shadow trapped in the Raysium dagger
**Origin:** Nalthis (Returned, Awakener), servant of the goddess Endowment

## Overview
The party's on-and-off captor, patron, and ultimately final boss. A Returned demigod from the shardworld Nalthis whose memories — including of a daughter — were forcibly stripped by Endowment. Charming, terrifying, and immune to conventional harm.

## Key Moments
- Kidnapped and poisoned the party after the fall of Vedenar; extracted "friendship favors" from multiple members over the following months.
- Ran the hidden fabrial-research lab **[[The Shallow Crypts]]** with [[Gavlin]], tasking him with building Stormlight-harvesting technology.
- True plan (uncovered via Gavlin's futuresight): gather the scattered pieces of Honor's power, absorb Odium's investiture instead, ascend to godhood, and kill Endowment in revenge.
- Immune to standard injury, Soulcasting, and physical attacks in the final battle; wielded a Shardblade and a "Daddy's Home" signature ability.
- Killed by [[Nucko]], who drove the recovered golden Raysium dagger into his chest, trapping his cognitive shadow in the pommel's gemstone.
