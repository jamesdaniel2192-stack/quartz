# Shai

**Status:** Alive
**Affiliation:** [[The Envisagers]]

## Overview
High priestess of the Envisager cult who captured the party at the start of the campaign, dueled [[Theren]], and holds knowledge of [[Kael]]'s missing mother.

## Key Moments
- Fled the party's first major fight after a duel with Theren.
- Reappeared in Kharbranth to taunt Kael about his mother's whereabouts, pointing him toward Yulay.
- Resurfaced unexpectedly at the meteorite ambush with a summoned Shardblade, executing the enemy Captain Ferris in a single strike — allegiance in that moment unclear.
