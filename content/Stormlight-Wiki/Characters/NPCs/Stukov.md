# Stukov

**Status:** Deceased. Bled out on his own ship in front of an audience, which is roughly the send-off he was asking for.

## Overview
Stukov was the racist, bigoted city lord of [[Kilna]], purchased as a prisoner off the dark-eyed revolt and dragged below decks for interrogation, at which point he immediately demonstrated exactly why an entire city had risen up against him. Given the microphone, so to speak, he used it to explain that "the Almighty chose us to lead," that dark-eyed people simply "have smaller brains," and that this was all, in his words, "just biology" — delivered with the calm confidence of a man who had never once been told no by anyone who mattered. He spent his entire captivity demanding to be freed and taunting the people holding the knives, which is a genuinely fascinating strategy for a man with no leverage, no army in reach for another hour, and rapidly diminishing hit points.

## Key Moments
- Taunted his captors relentlessly while chained below deck, repeating his views on racial hierarchy like they were a winning argument.
- Took a 17-damage punch from Nucko on 2 remaining HP, then a pen thrown clean through his forehead by Ravan — ink exploding across his face on the way to a brain wound serious enough that Gavlin had to sprint over and heal him just to keep him alive for further questioning.
- Woke up from that, saw a glowing-eyed Shardbearer standing over him, and immediately started screaming about voidbringers. A delightful man to the very end.
- Killed by [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]] via a shot near the femoral artery, after one taunt too many. Bled out on the spot in front of the other captured light-eyes. This wiki will not be observing a moment of silence.
