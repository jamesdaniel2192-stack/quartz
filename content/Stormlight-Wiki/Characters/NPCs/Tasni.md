# Tasni

**Status:** Deceased
**Role:** Leader of the Kilna dark-eyed revolt

## Overview
Rallied Kilna's dark-eyed population into open revolt against the light-eyes running the place, then had the good sense to recognize an actual pitched battle wasn't a winnable one — so instead he asked the party to buy his people just enough time to evacuate to the Reshi Isles.

## Key Moments
- Pulled [[Gavlin]], [[Ravan]], and [[Kael]] aside personally to lay out the evacuation plan — no middlemen, no theatrics, just a man asking strangers to save his people.
- Was visibly moved by Kael's rally speech, which held the dark-eyed line together just long enough for the evacuation to actually work.
- Later killed by [[Borash]] in a duel — mentioned only in passing, well after the fact, during the party's reflection on the whole Kilna mess. This wiki would like a fuller account of how that went. This wiki is not going to get one.
