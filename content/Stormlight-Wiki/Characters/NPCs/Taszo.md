# Taszo-son-Clutio

**Status:** Deceased
**Origin:** Shin, a Stone Shaman and budding Stoneward

## Overview
Freed from imprisonment aboard Sebarial's ship, Taszo joined the party and revealed key lore: the Shin have kept the Honorblades safe for millennia and are missing three of them, believing the Herald Taln has returned.

## Key Moments
- Died protecting the party during the meteorite ambush, struck down by Shai's Division surge.
- Passed with cryptic final words: "Tizzy... monastery..." and a plea to reclaim the stolen Honorblade for his people.
- Confirmed as a budding Stoneward when his spren departed his body.
