# Thack ("the Muffin Man") & Hansy

**Status:** Thack at large; Hansy recovered
**Affiliation:** Founder/figurehead of [[Church of the Yips]]

## Overview
Thack has stalked and harassed the party since Kharbranth (murdering an axehound breeder, planting a neurotoxin trap) and founded a full religious cult, the Church of the Yips, which venerates the Heralds as "Yip" spren.

Hansy (originally "Handsy") is Kael's stolen axehound — worshipped by the cult as "the Goodest Boy," the first Yip. Recovered by [[Sebarial]] and offered to the party as payment/bait near the campaign's end.

## Key Moments
- Party has burned down two Church of the Yips chapels hunting for Thack.
- Sebarial delivered Hansy back to the party at the final research outpost.
