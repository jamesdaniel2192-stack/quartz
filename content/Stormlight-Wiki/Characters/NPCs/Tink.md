# Tink

**Status:** Deceased

## Overview
Discovered as a stowaway hiding inside a water barrel mid-voyage — an entrance this wiki respects on principle — and promptly hired by [[Thack and Hansy|Thack]] for the princely sum of 8 marks to rig the axehound-breeder neurotoxin trap. Gullible, unstable, and locked in a one-sided rivalry with a man named "Chungledown Bim" who may or may not have deserved any of what followed.

## Key Moments
- Convinced by [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]] — falsely, and with zero pushback — that Highprince [[Marren]] was secretly his nemesis Chungledown Bim. That was, somehow, all it took.
- Delivered the actual killing blow on Marren in a later battle, slitting his throat while sincerely believing he'd finally gotten his rival, and claimed the Shardblade for himself.
- Killed almost immediately after by an enraged [[Theren]], deep in the grip of the Thrill, despite the rest of the party scrambling to save him. Theren took the Shardblade back. Tink got a wiki entry and a cautionary tale about believing everything Ravan tells you.
