# Vesper ("Blue")

**Status:** Alive, undercover
**Affiliation:** [[The Ghostbloods]]

## Overview
Nucko's high-ranking Ghostblood contact and romantic interest. First encountered as "Blue," a captive crew hand aboard Sebarial's ship — a deliberate cover to pass Nucko his companion doll, Pip.

## Key Moments
- Intercepted and is currently hiding an informant somewhere in Sebarial's War Camp — someone who holds the identities of the Ghostbloods who murdered Nucko's parents.
- Warned Nucko that [[Mraize]] was looking for him.
