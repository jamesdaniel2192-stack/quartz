# Young Jeber

**Also known as:** Young Jebber
**Status:** Deceased — and has apparently been that way "for a long time," which nobody clocked until it became relevant

## Overview
First crossed the wiki's radar as one of several marks Nucko pickpocketed during the cremling races in Kilna, alongside two similarly-and-confusingly-named associates, [[Old Jeber]] and [[Old Young Jeber]] — because apparently nobody in this family tree believes in unique names. Later revealed to be crew aboard Captain [[Sebarial]]'s ship, where [[Gavlin]] found him sporting a gruesome, bloodless gash across his stomach that reeked of alcohol instead of gore. Sebarial, entirely unbothered, confirmed the obvious: Young Jeber has actually been dead for a long time. Make of that what you will. This wiki has chosen not to ask follow-up questions.

## Key Moments
- Present and fully unconscious in Sebarial's Shallow Crypts hideout, sleeping straight through Gavlin's break-in to steal the Cephandrius letter from Sebarial's office. The dead sleep soundly, it turns out.
