# Gavlin (Ryan)

**Also known as:** —
**Status:** Alive
**Order:** Truthwatcher — "Enlightened" path (Second Ideal)
**Spren:** Glimmer in Doubt
**Mentor:** [[Rilano (Rolano)]]
**Family:** Brothers Harlan and Varden, in Highprince Aladar's camp

## Overview
An artifabrian and the party's resident genius, Gavlin spent much of the campaign embedded in [[Sebarial]]'s hidden lab, [[The Shallow Crypts]], building the very technology Sebarial intended to use to kill a god — before realizing it and turning against him.

## Oaths Sworn
- Second Ideal of the Truthwatchers — *"I will seek truth wherever it is hidden,"* sworn in the final battle against Sebarial.

## Key Moments
- Reunited with his mentor [[Rilano (Rolano)]] in Kharbranth and later worked alongside him in Sebarial's lab.
- Helped free a trapped spren from the "perfect gemstone" alongside [[Kael]] and [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]], meeting the Unmade **Sja-Anat**.
- His spren evolved into **Glimmer in Doubt**, gaining access to a futuresight talent tree (Stained-Glass Visions) after being touched by Odium's investiture.
- Used his visions to piece together Sebarial's true plan: absorbing Odium's power to kill the god Endowment.
- Discovered a torn letter from "Cephandrius" warning Sebarial off his plan.
- Built modern Roshar's first Surge-replicating fabrial and a Stormlight-harvesting reservoir.
- Widely voted campaign MVP for his nonstop healing output.

## Relationships
- [[Rilano (Rolano)]] — mentor, now a fellow fugitive from [[The Diagram]]
- [[Sebarial]] — patron and, ultimately, target
- Harlan & Varden — brothers with complicated feelings about Gavlin's path
