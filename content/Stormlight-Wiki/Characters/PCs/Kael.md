# Kael

**Also known as:** —
**Status:** Deceased — died fighting Sebarial, passed into the Cognitive Realm
**Order:** Willshaper (Third Ideal)
**Spren:** Cadenza
**Family:** Father [[Elias]] (Envisager cult leader), mother (held captive by the Envisagers), brother [[Jaro]]

## Overview
A former circus performer abused by his ringmaster Tan, Kael became the party's moral center and its heaviest hitter, wielding Cohesion (stone shaping — "sinkholing" was practically his catchphrase) with increasing mastery.

## Oaths Sworn
1. First Ideal — sworn early in the campaign during the first fights against the Envisagers.
2. Second Ideal (Willshaper) — *"I will seek freedom for those in bondage,"* sworn confronting his father [[Elias]] during "the Trolley Problem" (choosing between freeing Tan or saving his mother).
3. Third Ideal — *"I will be the master of my own chains, that my strength may serve the world instead of those who would bind it,"* sworn while bound and drained by [[Sebarial]] aboard his ship.

## Key Moments
- His spren Cadenza had secretly followed him since his circus days.
- Confronted his father [[Elias]] at the Envisager ritual site and learned [[Jaro]] was his brother and a cult plant.
- Built the party's entire secret underground lair beneath [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)|The Glyph Pool]] boutique using Stone Shaping.
- Died in the final assault on Sebarial's research outpost; in his last moments met the memory of his father as the warm man he'd been before things went wrong, and stepped into the Beyond.
