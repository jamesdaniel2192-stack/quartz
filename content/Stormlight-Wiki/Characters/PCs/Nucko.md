# Nucko (Caleb) — "The Button Thief"

**Also known as:** Nucco, "Jaro Guap" (heist alias — a terrible one, everyone saw through it immediately), Wayfarer, Buttons McGuap (unofficial, but earned)
**Status:** Alive. Statistically, this should not be true.
**Age:** Mid-30s. Nobody, including him, has ever pinned down an exact number.
**Affiliation:** [[The Ghostbloods]] (deep-cover operative, now rogue and hunted; RSVP status: complicated)
**Spren:** Masken
**Companion:** Pip, a sentient doll who has seen things and silently judges you for asking
**Parents:** Tovin & Marla — believed, for most of his life, to be Herdazian dock workers. Actually Scadrian off-worlders and Ghostbloods. See Origin.

## Overview
Somewhere on this storm-battered rock, a man tested a prison door that wasn't locked, immediately tripped the one tripwire in the room, and kicked off a four-priest firefight before anyone had finished waking up. That man was Nucko. This wiki entry exists because he survived that morning, and also every catastrophe since — including the discovery, well into adulthood, that the organization that raised him and the organization that orphaned him were, technically, the same organization.

Officially: a master of Illumination and Transformation, capable of bending light into illusions and soulcasting solid matter into something more convenient — usually water, occasionally a diversion, once, memorably, an entire museum's worth of alarm fabrials. Unofficially: a button-obsessed international criminal who also happens to be one of the most dangerous Radiants in the party, in between charming Ardents over shared appreciation of antique fasteners and lying to absolutely everyone, including — until fairly recently — the rest of the party.

**FILED UNDER CLASSIFIED, THEN LOUDLY DECLASSIFIED:** Nucko has been a fully initiated Ghostblood for most of his life and a double agent for a meaningful chunk of it, before announcing all of it mid-campaign — at which point several people quietly re-ran every conversation they'd ever had with him. Swore a binding blood oath with [[Kael]] on the spot afterward: any future deception is now, contractually, a death sentence.

## Origin

Nucko grew up in Herdaz believing his parents, Tovin and Marla, were exactly what they appeared to be: dock laborers and fishmongers, proud, poor, and fiercely protective of their only child. When he was five, he snuck out to play after they'd fallen asleep and forgot to blow out a candle on his way out. A breeze did the rest. The house burned; Tovin and Marla didn't make it out. Nucko did. He has disliked open flame ever since and lights his way almost exclusively with spheres — meaning the man who spent his childhood believing he'd burned his own parents alive now makes his living stealing the very light sources he depends on. He has never found this as funny as everyone who hears the story does.

Orphaned and homeless at five, Nucko learned fast that grief doesn't feed anyone. He stole fruit, then coins, then whole purses off drunk dockhands, and was self-taught in lockpicking, shadowing marks, and knowing exactly when to disappear well before anyone was checking in on him. At thirteen, breaking into a seamstress's house hunting for smuggled gemstones, he found a jar of buttons instead — and stole them anyway, because apparently this was always going to be his whole personality. Caught before he could fence them, he was dragged back to the seamstress, who laughed rather than pressed charges. The nickname stuck, and he leaned all the way into it: one button sewn onto his coat for every city, every job, every close call, until the coat became less a garment and more a scrapbook that occasionally deflects a knife.

Somewhere in those years on the street — the timeline is his and nobody's pinned it down more precisely than that — the Ghostbloods found him. And instead of doing whatever a lesser organization does with a homeless child who's already a better thief than most adults, they adopted him. For the rest of his life, as it turned out.

**What he didn't know, for a very long time:** Tovin and Marla weren't Herdazian dock workers. They were Scadrian off-worlders and Ghostbloods themselves, living deep undercover — and the fire that killed them wasn't the accident five-year-old Nucko spent his whole life blaming himself for. They were assassinated, by other Ghostbloods. [[Jernah, Halidar, and Melania]] were responsible; Jernah has since been spared, the other two are still at large. Nucko learned all of this as an adult, long after the same organization that killed his birth parents had already raised him as one of their own. He knows, logically, the candle wasn't the reason. It hasn't made much difference to how he feels about candles. It's made considerably more difference to how he feels about the Ghostbloods.

**Ongoing and unresolved:** lately, Nucko's been seeing something at the edge of his vision — there when he looks, gone the instant he rubs his eyes and looks again. It's spooked him out of at least one heist already. He assumes it's his eyesight failing, which would be refreshingly mundane, except he's personally stolen from every Eyesmith in the city at some point in his career, so getting it checked is not currently on the table. Filed under: problems entirely of his own making, a Nucko specialty. See also: [[Open Threads]].

And if you cross him? Your coinpurse goes missing, your back door ends up unlocked, and — because the man has never once resisted the urge to sign his work — a single, colorful button turns up somewhere it shouldn't be.

## Key Moments — Now With Considerably More Evidence

### The Cons & Heists
- **Session 1, minute three:** found the cell door unlocked, tripped the tripwire anyway. Still the single most accurate data point on this entire page about how Nucko operates.
- **The Museum Job:** cracked all five alarm fabrials at the Kharbranth Button Museum, soulcast a display case into water, and walked out with a Herald's actual buttons and an entire historical gown — all while charming an Ardent named Lia so thoroughly that natural 20s were rolled and the table audibly reacted. Set the place on fire on the way out; technically [[Theren]]'s doing, but Nucko was standing right there, delighted.
- **The duel nobody won:** a mutual pickpocketing standoff with [[Jaro]] over a stolen button, called a tie. Possibly the most honorable moment in the entire campaign.
- **The Button Blunder:** aborted a heist at Sensible Curiosities after soulcasting a window into water and immediately spotting a hidden alarm fabrial he definitely should have checked for first. [[Torin]] flatly refused to cut a hole in the wall with his Shardblade "for something so trivial." The shop survived. Barely.
- **The Voice of Authority:** impersonated a high lord in the middle of a warzone to order a fake retreat — and an entire company of enemy troops turned and fled because Nucko told them to. No one has explained how this worked. It just did.

### The Chaos, Purely Uncut
- Mid-escape from a burning warehouse he'd helped set on fire, sprinted back *into* the danger zone because he suddenly needed chouta, and got it, on the strength of shouting "the chulls are beautiful this time of year!" at a visibly traumatized food vendor.
- Extorted a nineteen-year-old girl for payment in exchange for freeing her captured father. She stabbed him for it. [[Ravan]] pulled the knife back out. This is, somehow, filed under "downtime."
- Attempted to case a grieving shopkeeper's store for a burglary literally minutes after the party discovered a warehouse full of murdered parshmen down the street. Rolled a natural 1. Got threatened with the city guard. Fully earned.
- Threw [[Kael]] under the bus in an official party debate over "worst cook in the group," on the logic that the strongest fighter definitely can't cook. No supporting evidence offered, none needed.
- Picked [[Gavlin]] as his arm-wrestling target specifically because Gavlin's Strength score is zero. Arguably the most sound tactical decision Nucko has ever made.

### Combat, Occasionally Successful
- Got absolutely flattened by a whitespine — critical hit, tusk embedded in his bicep, knocked unconscious before he could get a word in — then came back later in the same fight arc to finish an 800-pound whitespine off by turning it into solid stone. Not always the hero. Usually still gets the kill.
- Broke out of a battlefield grapple mid-fight and soulcast his own attacker into liquid blood, on-screen, to demoralize the surrounding troops, then reclaimed the stolen gemstones from what used to be the guy.
- Lost his own doll, Pip, to an enemy soldier who intercepted 7,000 of his marks and punted the doll thirty feet across the battlefield. [[Dblar]] tackled the grunt to break the grapple; Nucko drew a stolen sword, finished the soldier off, then dropped a second enemy commander to half health with a raw Soulcasting burst. Pip was recovered. Pip has opinions about this. Pip's opinions were not solicited.
- Passed a critical Thievery check to relieve the campaign's nearly unkillable final boss of the one weapon that could actually hurt him — mid-fight, while said boss was actively trying to murder everyone. [[Sebarial]] was immune to physical damage, standard injury, and Soulcasting. He was not immune to pickpocketing.
- Executed a captured Ghostblood spy alongside [[Torin]] in a synchronized flurry, then soulcast the entire corpse into clean air, leaving nothing for anyone to find. Efficient. Deeply unsettling. On brand.

### The Reckonings
- Interrogated Jernah — one of the three Ghostbloods responsible for his real parents' deaths — with a brutal Intimidation check until he broke and confessed everything. Spared his life anyway, with a warning that the mercy would not repeat.
- Discovered that Blue, a prisoner being held aboard Sebarial's ship, was actually [[Vesper (Blue)]] — his handler, contact, and romantic interest — who'd let herself get captured on purpose just to smuggle Pip back to him.
- Confessed, unprompted, to the entire party that he'd been a lifelong Ghostblood double agent — landing on top of the earlier reveal about his actual parentage and the organization that raised him. Somehow survived both conversations. Swore the blood oath with [[Kael]] immediately after.
- **Killed [[Sebarial]]** — drove the stolen Raysium dagger into the demigod's chest himself, trapping his cognitive shadow inside its gemstone for good, and closing out the campaign's central antagonist. The guy who tripped over a tripwire in Session 1 personally ended the final boss.
- Forged a fake copy of *The Book of the Returned* with [[Mraize]] to feed Sebarial false intel on the location of Odium's Perpendicularity, misdirecting him toward the mountains of Shinovar instead of the actual site on the Shattered Plains.

## Relationships
- Tovin & Marla — his birth parents, and the reason he doesn't like fire. Everything he believed about them growing up was sincere; not all of it was accurate.
- [[The Ghostbloods]] — took him in off the street as a child and raised him. Also, through other members entirely, responsible for his parents' deaths. Nucko contains multitudes, and also grievances.
- [[Theren]] — former Ghostblood partner, closest friend, mutual enabler of arson
- [[Kael]] — blood-oath bound; trust rebuilding, slowly, under contractual duress
- [[Ravan]] — party's other resident hothead; the two have grappled each other at least once purely out of principle
- [[Torin]] — reliable in a fight, correctly refuses to use his Shardblade for button heists
- [[Vesper (Blue)]] — Ghostblood contact and romantic interest, complicated in the specific way everything involving Nucko is complicated
- [[Mraize]] — professional alliance held together entirely by mutual usefulness
- Jernah, Halidar, and Melania — his parents' killers. Jernah spared. Two still at large. Unresolved, and everyone at the table knows it.
