# Nucko (Caleb) — "The Button Thief"

**Also known as:** Nucco, "Jaro Guap" (heist alias — a terrible one, everyone saw through it immediately), Wayfarer, Buttons McGuap (unofficial, but earned)
**Status:** Alive. Statistically, this should not be true.
**Affiliation:** [[The Ghostbloods]] (deep-cover operative, now rogue and hunted; RSVP status: complicated)
**Spren:** Masken
**Companion:** Pip, a sentient doll who has seen things and silently judges you for asking
**Raised believing:** he was a Herdazian orphan named Nucko
**Later learned:** he wasn't. See below.

## Overview
Somewhere on this storm-battered rock, a man tested a prison door that wasn't locked, immediately tripped the one tripwire in the room, and kicked off a four-priest firefight before anyone had finished waking up. That man was Nucko. This wiki entry exists because he survived that morning, and also every catastrophe since — including the fairly significant discovery, partway through his own life, that the tragedy he thought defined him wasn't the whole story.

Officially: a master of Illumination and Transformation, capable of bending light into illusions and soulcasting solid matter into something more convenient — usually water, occasionally a diversion, once, memorably, an entire museum's worth of alarm fabrials. Unofficially: a button-obsessed international criminal who also happens to be one of the most dangerous Radiants in the party, in between charming Ardents over shared appreciation of antique fasteners and lying to absolutely everyone, including — until fairly recently — the rest of the party.

**FILED UNDER CLASSIFIED, THEN LOUDLY DECLASSIFIED:** Nucko spent twenty-five-plus years as a fully initiated Ghostblood double agent before announcing it mid-campaign, at which point several people quietly re-ran every conversation they'd ever had with him. Swore a binding blood oath with [[Kael]] on the spot afterward — any future deception is now, contractually, a death sentence.

## Origin — Two Versions, Neither of Them Gentle

This wiki does not have the authority to tell you which of Nucko's tragedies is the "real" one. As far as we can tell, they're both real. He's just been carrying two of them.

**The story he grew up on.** Nucko's earliest memories are the sea-salted winds of Herdaz and a small, warm house where Tovin and Marla — dock laborers, fishmongers, proud, poor, and fiercely protective — raised him as their son. When Nucko was five, he snuck out after they'd fallen asleep to play with friends, and forgot to blow out a candle on his way. It sat on the windowsill, well clear of the drapes, right up until a breeze disagreed. By the time he made it back, the house was gone, and so were Tovin and Marla. He has disliked open flame ever since and lights his way almost exclusively with spheres — which means the man who accidentally burned his own family alive now makes his living stealing the very light sources he's had to depend on since. Nucko has never found this as funny as everyone who hears the story does.

Orphaned, homeless, and five years old, he learned quickly that grief doesn't feed anyone. He stole fruit, then coins, then whole purses off drunk dockhands, and by six was self-taught in lockpicking, shadowing marks, and knowing exactly when to vanish. At thirteen, breaking into a seamstress's house hunting for smuggled gemstones, he found a jar of buttons instead — and stole them anyway, because apparently this was always going to be his whole personality. Caught before he could fence them, he was dragged back to the seamstress, who laughed rather than pressed charges. The nickname stuck. Nucko, showing the branding instincts that would go on to define him, leaned all the way in: one button sewn onto his coat for every city, every job, every close call, until the coat became less a garment and more a scrapbook that occasionally deflects a knife.

**The story that turned out to be true.** Somewhere along the way — after enough years, enough jobs, enough reasons to start asking questions the Ghostbloods didn't want asked — Nucko learned that this wasn't actually where he came from. His real family were off-worlders from Scadrial, and they were killed by the Ghostbloods, who then folded him into their ranks as a child. Whatever role the fire, or Tovin and Marla, or any of it actually played in that story is something even this wiki can't confirm. What's confirmed is who's responsible: [[Jernah, Halidar, and Melania]] were involved in his family's deaths. Jernah has since been spared. The other two are still out there, and Nucko has not forgotten.

Whichever version is doing the heavy lifting on a given day, the guilt runs the same either way — it's just aimed at a different target depending on how recently he's thought about it. Now thirty, he's a legend in the lower city and, unbeknownst to most of it, one of its more dangerous residents. Lighthearted and charming in public, always working an angle; in private, still trying to figure out which of his families he's actually allowed to grieve. He's spent his whole life looking for a new one, and once the party proved they'd stick around, he did the thing he apparently always does: leaned all the way in.

**Ongoing and unresolved:** lately, Nucko's been seeing something at the edge of his vision — there when he looks, gone the instant he rubs his eyes and looks again. It's spooked him out of at least one heist already. He assumes it's his eyesight failing, which would be refreshingly mundane, except he's personally stolen from every Eyesmith in the city at some point in his career, so getting it checked is not currently on the table. Filed under: problems entirely of his own making, a Nucko specialty. See also: [[Open Threads]].

And if you cross him? Your coinpurse goes missing, your back door ends up unlocked, and — because the man has never once resisted the urge to sign his work — a single, colorful button turns up somewhere it shouldn't be.

## Key Moments — A Highlight Reel, Because What Else Would You Call This

- **Session 1, minute three:** found the cell door unlocked, tripped the tripwire anyway. Still the single most accurate data point on this entire page about how Nucko operates.
- **The interrogation that wasn't:** the party spent real, careful effort intimidating, deceiving, and persuading a captured priest for information. Nucko killed him anyway. The transcript needs no further commentary.
- **Swore his First Ideal** on the Vedenar docks disguised as [[Theren]]'s father, specifically to scare off [[Marren]]. It worked. The Knights Radiant selection process remains a mystery to everyone, including, presumably, the Almighty.
- **The Museum Job:** cracked all five alarm fabrials at the Kharbranth Button Museum, soulcast a display case into water, and walked out with a Herald's actual buttons and an entire historical gown — all while charming an Ardent named Lia so thoroughly that natural 20s were rolled and the table audibly reacted. Set the place on fire on the way out; technically [[Theren]]'s doing, but Nucko was standing right there, delighted — which, given everything above, is either deeply on-brand or deeply concerning, take your pick.
- **The duel nobody won:** a mutual pickpocketing standoff with Jaro over a stolen button, called a tie. Possibly the most honorable moment in the entire campaign.
- **The Reveal:** confessed, unprompted, to being a Ghostblood operative the whole time — on top of the other reveal, about his actual origins, that had already landed by this point. Very high stakes. Very healthy relationship dynamic.
- **Killed [[Sebarial]]** — drove the Raysium dagger in himself, trapping his cognitive shadow for good. The button thief closed out the campaign's main antagonist. Nobody called that back in Session 1, least of all the guy who tripped over a tripwire.
- Forged a fake copy of *The Book of the Returned* with [[Mraize]] to mislead Sebarial about Odium's Perpendicularity. Genuinely impressive scale of deception for a man whose signature move is "steal the buttons."
