# Nucko (Caleb)

**Also known as:** Nucco, "Jaro Guap" (heist alias), Wayfarer
**Status:** Alive
**Affiliation:** [[The Ghostbloods]] (deep cover, now rogue)
**Spren:** Masken
**Companion:** Pip (a sentient doll)

## Overview
A charming, chaotic button-obsessed thief who started the campaign as comic relief and turned out to be one of its biggest secrets. Master of Illumination (light-weaving/illusions) and Transformation (Soulcasting), usually deployed for heists, cons, or setting things dramatically on fire alongside [[Theren]].

## Key Moments
- Swore his First Ideal of the Knights Radiant on the Vedenar docks, disguising himself as Theren's father to scare off [[Marren]].
- Master thief of the campaign: robbed the Kharbranth Button Museum, cracked Taravangian's vault, stole from half the NPCs in the game.
- **The Mega-Reveal:** confessed to being a fully initiated Ghostblood double agent for 25+ years, with [[Theren]] as his original operative partner.
- Revealed his parents were off-worlders from Scadrial killed by local [[The Ghostbloods|Ghostbloods]], who framed him as a child and raised him into their ranks.
- Swore a binding Blood Oath with [[Kael]] after the reveal — any future deception is a death sentence.
- Landed the killing blow on [[Sebarial]], driving the Raysium dagger into his chest and trapping his cognitive shadow.
- Orchestrated the forged copy of *The Book of the Returned* with [[Mraize]] to mislead Sebarial about Odium's Perpendicularity.

## Relationships
