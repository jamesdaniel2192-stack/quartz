# Theren

**Also known as:** Theron, "Gwap" (heist alias)
**Status:** Deceased — died in battle, body Soulcast to stone
**Affiliation:** Highprince Valam's army; formerly [[The Ghostbloods]]
**Spren:** Geoffrey (bond broken before death)
**Family:** Father (murdered by [[Marren]]), younger brother [[Torin]]

## Overview
A skilled duelist and troublemaker with a flair for arson, Theren was disgraced when his family was declared traitors to the Veden Crown. He rose to command his own battalion under Highprince Valam and eventually became the campaign's most dramatic casualty.

## Key Moments
- Swore the First Ideal of the Knights Radiant defending [[Kael]]'s dog from [[Shai]] in the very first arc.
- Learned his father was dead and his family branded traitors; responded with total indifference ("eh I hated him anyway").
- Confronted and grappled [[Marren]], who killed his father and stole the family Shardblade.
- Given command of a battalion by Highprince Valam; led the breach of Vedenar's walls.
- Increasingly embraced **the Thrill** during the siege of Vedenar, eventually breaking his bond with Geoffrey and becoming a **Thrill Champion**.
- Killed Tink in a rage after Tink slew Marren and claimed the family Shardblade for himself.
- **Revealed post-mortem** to have been a 25-year Ghostblood double agent alongside [[Nucko]].
- Died in the battle before the party's arrival in New Natan; body recovered and turned to stone by Soulcasting, honored as "Captain General High Prince Doctor Knight Radiant Captain Theron."

## Relationships
- [[Nucko]] — Ghostblood partner and closest friend
- [[Torin]] — younger brother, who took up his cause afterward
- [[Marren]] — rival, father's killer
