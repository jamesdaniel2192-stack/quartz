# Torin

**Also known as:** Torrin, "Joran" (heist alias)
**Status:** Alive
**Family:** Younger brother of [[Theren]]
**Companions:** Shield (Ryshadium horse), Flick (ferret, first confirmed kill of the campaign)
**Weapon:** A Grandbow, gifted by [[Sebarial]]

## Overview
Introduced shortly after [[Theren]]'s death, Torin stepped into the party as his brother's successor — initially refusing to take the family Shardblade out of respect, eventually helping decide its fate alongside the party.

## Key Moments
- Present for the decision on what to do with Theren's Shardblade; ultimately agreed to let [[Jaro]] hold onto it.
- Formally received the Grandbow from [[Sebarial]] as part of a trade for Red Thorn seeds.
- Went undercover as "Joran" alongside Ravan ("Gellis") to establish the party's front business, [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)|The Glyph Pool]].
- Struck up (and briefly wrecked, then repaired) a romance with [[Liss]], a clerical worker in the Kholin War Camps.
- Burned down a Church of the Yips chapel and sniped its stained-glass window from 600 yards with a bomb arrow.

## Relationships
- [[Theren]] — older brother
- [[Liss]] — romantic interest, now officially courting
- [[Sebarial]] — complicated; benefactor and threat
