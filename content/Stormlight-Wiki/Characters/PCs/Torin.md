# Torin — Custodian of Complicated Inheritances

**Also known as:** Torrin, "Joran" (heist alias — considerably more convincing than Nucko's "Jaro Guap," which is a genuinely low bar to clear)
**Status:** Alive. Still traveling with the party, which at this point in the campaign's body count is basically a promotion.
**Family:** Younger brother of [[Theren]] *(deceased)*, later revealed to have spent 25 years as a Ghostblood double agent alongside [[Nucko]] — a fact Torin learned the same evening as everyone else, standing right there for the whole mega-reveal.
**Companions:** Shield (Ryshadium horse — "a very good boi," no roll required), Flick (ferret, first confirmed kill of the campaign, gold-tooth disposal specialist)
**Weapon:** A Grandbow, gifted by [[Sebarial]] *(deceased)* in trade for the party's Red Thorn seeds. Also, apparently, occasionally referred to as a Shardblade by people who were there. The wiki has given up trying to reconcile this. See below.
**Romance:** [[Liss]], a Kholin War Camps clerical worker — survived Torin forgetting her name mid-cover-story to become officially, contractually courting.

## Overview
Every party needs someone who is not, technically, capable of summoning a god-forged killing machine out of the Cognitive Realm on command — someone whose combat contribution is a very good bow, a very good horse, and a ferret with a taste for noses. That someone is Torin. He stepped into the group on the worst night of his life, holding a grudge against a magic sword and a healthy amount of suspicion toward literally everyone in it, and somehow ended up the one still standing at the campfire while two of the people who welcomed him in did not make it. He is, technically, un-infused — no Surges, no spren, no easy out when the world turns molecularly hostile — which has meant a level of exhaustion on the Frostlands march, an afternoon spent completely immobilized by a compressor fabrial, and at least one catapult shot straight to the face. He kept showing up anyway.

Officially: a marksman with a longbow, later a Grandbow, later occasionally implied to also be a Shardblade depending on who's narrating that session. Unofficially: the guy who refuses to use his family's priceless magic sword to cut a hole in a wall for a button heist "because it's beneath it," then turns around and burns down two separate religious buildings for the exact same friend group. Consistency is a spectrum. He loves his horse, adores his ferret, and appears constitutionally incapable of remembering a woman's name after two weeks of forced marches — a flaw that has cost him dearly and which he has, against all odds, worked to fix.

## Origin
Torin's introduction to the party doubles as the worst night any of them had had up to that point. Moments after the battle that killed [[Theren]], [[Nucko]] stood over his brother's body holding the recovered family Shardblade and, upon meeting Torin for the first time, offered it to him outright. Torin refused — calling it a symbol of his family's corruption, a line the table has never gotten more detail on, and this wiki isn't going to pretend it invented any. Whatever happened between Torin and his family, and whatever that blade actually represented before it ever touched Theren's hand, stayed unsaid. He eventually accepted the weapon anyway, not out of forgiveness or reconciliation with whatever it symbolized, but to keep it out of worse hands. [[Kael]], meeting a total stranger being handed a priceless Shardblade, was audibly not thrilled about it and threatened to end Torin personally if he ever abused the weapon. Torin agreed with him on the spot. The party debated the whole arrangement and ultimately settled on letting [[Jaro]] hold onto the blade — which has not stopped Torin from being the one repeatedly recorded carrying, refusing to use, and getting threatened over a Shardblade in every session since. Custody, on this wiki, is a theory. Possession is a fact.

A few days later, Theren's body was brought aboard ship, posed, and turned permanently to stone via Soulcasting. Torin sat with it. When it came time to say something, he didn't reach for anything grand: "I'll remember you as the good brother you were when I was a kid." No footnote necessary. Some lines just get to stand there.

## Key Moments

### An Inheritance Nobody Can Fully Explain
- Refused Theren's Shardblade on sight, on the grounds that it represented his family's corruption — a sentence the campaign has never once elaborated on, and which this wiki is choosing to respect rather than invent an answer for.
- Accepted the blade anyway specifically to keep it away from anyone who'd actually use it, prompting Kael to threaten his life over it within minutes of meeting him. Torin, remarkably, agreed that was fair.
- Present for the party's formal ruling that the blade would go to [[Jaro]] for safekeeping — and then went right on being the one everyone remembers holding, refusing to swing, and getting menaced over it. Someone in this campaign is keeping bad notes, and it might be the Shardblade itself.
- Flatly refused to let Nucko use it to slice a convenient hole in the wall of Sensible Curiosities during a button heist, on the grounds that using an ancestral weapon of unknown moral weight for petty larceny was "something so trivial." The building survived. Barely.
- Cornered by the Ghostblood spy Kel in the woods outside camp, who mocked him for the blade and threatened to weaponize Torin's "high-eyes lineage" as blackmail. Torin's answer was an arrow to the back, no hesitation, no follow-up questions.

### The Business of Being "Joran"
- Went undercover with [[Ravan]] (posing as the Reshi merchant "Gellis") to secure the party's front business, [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)|The Glyph Pool]] — tanked the official registry leadership check with a rolled 2, but recovered enough charm to talk clerk [[Liss]] into a friendly discount and her tavern schedule.
- Insisted their deckhand [[Dblar]] be kept entirely out of the loop on the shop's underground lair and its finances — a call that, given everyone else's track record with secrets, was probably the correct one.
- Rode Shield to help Kael lay defensive traps faster during the frantic 45-minute prep for Operation Defend Kilna. No roll required. Shield, once again, a very good boi.

### The Romance of Torin & Liss
- Reconnected with Liss at the tavern mid-cover-op, immediately forgot her name from sheer exhaustion, and tried to cover by introducing Flick. Liss saw through it instantly — she'd worked out he'd only romanced her for a cheap business license — and threw him out in tears in front of a Kholin guard, who put a hand on his sword hilt for good measure. Torin's parting move: having Flick pickpocket a single mark off the guard on the way out the door, because apparently priorities remain priorities even during a public heartbreak.
- Watched Liss rebound directly into Kael's lap the second he left the room, culminating in Kael carving her a creepy little stone statue and setting up a date to buy illegal Horn Eater White. Somehow this worked in Kael's favor.
- Tracked Liss back down at the Ornery Chull with a bouquet and a pair of custom shoes hand-crafted by [[Gavlin]]. Got caught in a small lie about whether he'd stitched them himself — and won her over anyway by admitting, honestly, that he'd just wanted to impress her.
- Secured a 200-mark private dinner, officially became her courting partner, and personally ended Kael's rebound plans in the process. The wiki does not know how Kael feels about this. The wiki suspects "fine, mostly."

### Blood and Bowstrings
- Landed a clean longbow shot square between a White Spine's shoulder blades during the temple trial gauntlet, doing his part to help take down four horse-sized monsters in a stone corridor.
- Claimed the Yu-Nerig greatshell as his personal quarry and put three unrelenting longbow shots into the 20-foot aquatic monster while the rest of the party lit it on fire, turned it to stone, and generally made a production of it.
- Fell completely immobilized for three full turns during the meteorite ambush when enemy compressor fabrials seized his armor solid — a distinctly un-fun reminder of what fighting alongside Radiants looks like when you aren't one.
- Test-drove his brand-new Grandbow against an enemy Skybreaker mid-battle, rolled with disadvantage from his strength deficit, and completely whiffed the shot. Crestfallen, he put the bow away to nurse his bruised ego. The Grandbow's win rate remains a work in progress.
- Charged into the enemy on Shield during the meteorite ambush, trampling a cluster of burning soldiers — and, as a side effect, accidentally stomping out Nucko's fire attack mid-battle. Friendly fire, four legs.
- Watched Flick secure the ferret's first official kill of the campaign, biting a soldier's nose clean off from Shield's flank for the finishing blow.
- Took a direct hit from an enemy catapult during the same fight and went down unconscious for the rest of the session — the cost of being the one party member without a healing Surge on standby.
- In the campaign's final confrontation with Sebarial, charged the demigod on Shield as he took Kael hostage; notched bomb arrows to collapse the facility's external support towers while the rest of the party stripped Sebarial of his weapon and ended him for good.

### Arson, Twice — Keep These Straight
- **At the Kholin war-camp Church of the Yips chapel**, alongside Kael and Gavlin: passed a 19 Survival check to find fresh axehound paw prints proving the escaped Hansy had been through recently, then rifled the private office for a hierarchy chart of the Nine Seneschals, 100 marks, and an ear trumpet before dousing the bookshelves in Horn Eater White and torching the top floor. Snuck downstairs afterward acting completely oblivious as smoke poured in behind him, letting the chapel's own guardian take the blame for a "candle left burning."
- **At the second Church of the Yips chapel, in Highprince Aladar's camp**, alongside Kael and Nucko: waited as Kael bypassed the locked door with stone shaping, set the building ablaze from the inside, then tunneled the party out beneath the foundation. From 600 yards away, Torin notched a bomb arrow and put a bullseye clean through the remaining stained-glass window, bringing the entire structure down. Two chapels. Two different fires. Zero regrets on record for either.

### Reckonings, Deceptions, and a Weirdly Good Poker Face
- Rolled a natural 20 on Deception to cover for a visibly sweating Nucko during the Gold Tooth Incident, convincing a suspicious Sebarial that Torin possessed "the innocence of an honest man" — a genuinely funny thing for anyone to say about a man currently operating under a false name.
- One of only two party members to shake off Sebarial's poison unassisted the night the crew was kidnapped, thanks to another well-timed natural 20.
- Warned the party, correctly and pointedly, that Sebarial had never once entered a city without already having eyes and ears on the ground — a read on the man's paranoia that turned out to matter.
- Walked back from executing the Ghostblood spy Kel and talked Nucko through whether Jernah, one of his parents' three killers, deserved to live — reasoning that if Jernah was genuinely remorseful and no longer a threat, living with the guilt was punishment enough. Then agreed to help Nucko hunt down the two killers who were still out there.
- Alongside Nucko, was openly hesitant about handing the Unmade Sja-Anat her trapped spren's gemstone, pointing out — reasonably — that the party's last brush with an Unmade ended with his brother dead and their closest ally turned briefly homicidal by the Thrill.
- Present for [[Nucko]]'s farewell, alongside [[Gavlin]] and [[Ravan]], when Nucko sat the three of them down for a real goodbye before handing Gavlin the Raysium dagger and walking off down the road with [[Vesper (Blue)]]. No disguise, no vanishing act — just one more person Torin has had to watch leave.

## Relationships
- [[Theren]] *(deceased)* — older brother, remembered fondly despite whatever "family corruption" the shardblade represents. Torin's last words to his stone-turned body: "I'll remember you as the good brother you were when I was a kid." Later revealed to have been a 25-year Ghostblood double agent, which somehow hasn't touched that memory.
- [[Liss]] — romantic interest turned officially courting partner, after surviving Torin forgetting her name in public exactly once.
- [[Sebarial]] *(deceased)* — complicated; benefactor, kidnapper, gold-tooth interrogator, and ultimately the campaign's central threat. Killed by Nucko in the final confrontation Torin fought through.
- [[Nucko]] — fellow original party member, partner in the woods ambush against Jernah's killers, one of the three Nucko sat down with to say a proper goodbye before departing the party for good.
- [[Kael]] *(deceased)* — threatened to kill Torin the night they met over the Shardblade question, then spent the rest of the campaign as a reliable ally and occasional rebound rival for Liss's attention. Died in the same battle that ended Sebarial.
- [[Gavlin]] — crafted the custom shoes that helped win Liss back; one of the three present for Nucko's farewell.
- [[Ravan]] — partner in the "Joran & Gellis" front-business con and the woods ambush against Jernah's killers; one of the three present for Nucko's farewell.
- [[Jaro]] — officially holds Theren's family Shardblade per party decision, though the session record keeps putting it in Torin's hands anyway.
