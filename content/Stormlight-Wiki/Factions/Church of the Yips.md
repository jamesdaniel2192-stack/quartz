# Church of the Yips

**Status:** Active, multiple chapels (two destroyed by the party)
**Founder:** [[Thack and Hansy|Thack ("the Muffin Man")]]

## Overview
A religious cult venerating the Heralds as unique "Yip" spren, with Kael's stolen dog Hansy elevated to living demigod status ("the Goodest Boy," first of the Yips). Run through a hierarchy of "Nine Seneschals."

## History with the Party
- Chapel in the Kholin War Camps and a second chapel in Highprince Aladar's camp have both been burned down by the party while hunting Thack and Hansy.
- Guardian **Shifty** remains an unwitting party contact within the church.
