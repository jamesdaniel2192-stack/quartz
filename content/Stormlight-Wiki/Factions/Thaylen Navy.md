# Thaylen Navy

**Status:** Neutral / ally-adjacent — the rare faction on this wiki that has not, at any point, tried to kidnap, imprison, or assassinate anyone

## Overview

Showed up during Dblar's smuggler skirmish and actually helped, then handed the party a Letter of Marque on top of it — a document with enough real political weight behind it that even [[Sebarial]] would have coveted one. Genuine, uncomplicated goodwill from an official state navy is rare enough in this campaign that it's worth noting in full: they intervened, they didn't ask for anything sketchy in return, and nobody has had to burn a chapel or fake their own capture because of them since. This wiki intends to enjoy it while it lasts.
