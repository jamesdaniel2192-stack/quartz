# The Diagram

**Status:** Active, antagonistic
**Key Members:** [[Dahl (Nico)|Dahl]], [[Rilano (Rolano)|Rilano]] (former agent, defected)

## Overview
A secretive, utilitarian organization obsessed with philosophical calculus (sacrificing the few for the many) and deeply interested in Radiant Ideals and the party's growing powers.

## History with the Party
- Interrogated and imprisoned [[Ravan (Amanrava)|Ravan]] under the alias "Wot"/"Doll."
- Ordered a hunt on [[Rilano (Rolano)|Rilano]] after he tried to abandon the organization.
- Ambushed the party at the meteorite crash site with advanced Kharbranthian fabrial soldiers (Drainers, compressor "stiff armor" units).
- [[Dahl (Nico)|Dahl]] resurfaced alive in New Natan despite being presumed dead.
