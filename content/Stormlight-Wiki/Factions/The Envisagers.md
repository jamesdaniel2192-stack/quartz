# The Envisagers

**Status:** Leadership scattered, ideology stubbornly persists (as ideology does)
**Key Members:** [[Shai]] *(high priestess, professionally hard to keep caught)*, [[Elias]] *(architect, also — inconveniently — Kael's father)*, [[Jaro]] *(former mole)*

## Overview

The cult that started this whole mess: they drugged and kidnapped the party in the campaign's opening act, dragging everyone down into a Bavland dungeon to run rituals meant to summon power and put their own initiates in genuine danger. This is, as far as the wiki's records go, ground zero — the origin point tangled up with how the party's spren bonds actually began. Every other faction on this page is, in some sense, downstream of what happened in that dungeon.

## History with the Party

- Opening arc: the party fought their way out of the Envisager dungeon outright, killing the priest guards and tearing apart the ritual hall on the way. Not a subtle exit.
- [[Shai]] escaped that initial confrontation and has had the poor manners to keep resurfacing ever since.
- [[Kael]]'s own father, [[Elias]], turned out to be a mastermind behind the ritual site — the kind of family reveal that makes holiday dinners considerably more complicated.
- [[Jaro]] was unmasked as a longtime embedded agent, tracking Kael specifically, the entire time everyone thought he was just around.
- [[Nale]] deputized the party to go hunt down the Envisagers' remaining hideouts — Dumadari, Kharbranth, Yulay — turning the group that once fled this cult's dungeon into the people now kicking down its doors.
