# The Envisagers

**Status:** Leadership scattered, ideology persists
**Key Members:** [[Shai]] (high priestess), [[Elias]] (architect), [[Jaro]] (former mole)

## Overview
The cult that drugged and kidnapped the party at the very start of the campaign, running dangerous rituals in a Bavland dungeon meant to summon power and put initiates in danger — closely tied to the origins of the party's spren bonds.

## History with the Party
- Opening arc: the party fought their way out of the Envisager dungeon, killing the priest guards and disrupting the ritual hall.
- [[Shai]] escaped the initial confrontation and resurfaced multiple times afterward.
- [[Kael]]'s father [[Elias]] was revealed as a mastermind behind the ritual site.
- [[Jaro]] was revealed as a longtime embedded agent tracking Kael.
- [[Nale]] deputized the party to hunt down remaining Envisager hideouts (Dumadari, Kharbranth, Yulay).
