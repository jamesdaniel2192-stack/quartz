# The Ghostbloods

**Status:** Active; being undermined from within
**Local Leader:** [[Mraize]]
**Key Members:** [[Nucko]] (rogue double agent), [[Theren]] (deceased former agent), Vesper ("Blue"), Jernah, Halidar, Melania

## Overview
A far-reaching secret intelligence organization. Their local Rosharan cell murdered Nucko's off-world Scadrial parents 30 years ago and raised him as an unwitting embedded agent alongside Theren.

## History with the Party
- Secretly engineered many of the party's early "coincidental" plot detours: the Vedenar boat theft, the diversion toward Kharbranth, the button factory fire (to steal Voidbringer-hunting spores), even the tracking of Hansy's axehound pups.
- Nucko's mega-reveal blew this history open, prompting a Blood Oath with [[Kael]].
- Nucko is now working with [[Mraize]] on a forged copy of *The Book of the Returned* to mislead [[Sebarial]] about Odium's Perpendicularity.
