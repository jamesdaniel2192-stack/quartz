# Bavland

**Type:** Mining settlement
**Claim to fame:** Ground zero. Literally where this campaign started.
**Notable landmark:** An underground ritual dungeon nobody consented to being drugged and imprisoned in

## Overview
Bavland is where it all began, in the least metaphorical sense possible: a mining settlement that also happened to be sitting on top of an underground dungeon run by [[The Envisagers]] for whatever their rituals required, and the place the entire party woke up drugged and imprisoned in before Session 1 had even finished introducing itself. Somewhere in that cell block is where [[Nucko]] tested a supposedly locked door, found it open, tripped the one tripwire in the room anyway, and kicked off a four-priest firefight roughly three minutes into everyone's very first session — setting the tone for absolutely everything that followed.

Above ground, Bavland's claim to normalcy was [[Leif]]'s original tavern — presumably a perfectly nice place, before its regulars started disappearing into a ritual basement.
