# Kharbranth

## Overview
A major city and one of the campaign's central hubs. Home to the Conclave (library/administration), the Button Museum (robbed and burned by [[Nucko]] and [[Theren]]), [[Rilano (Rolano)|Rilano]]'s fabrial lab, and Taravangian's palace — site of a vault heist and Theren's near-capture by the shardbearer Graves. The Glyphpool tavern is a recurring meeting spot here.

## Key Events
- The Button Museum heist and arson.
- Taravangian's vault infiltration; the party discovered a copy of the Diagram.
- Confrontation in Taravangian's throne room; Graves the shardbearer grappled Theren.
