# Kharbranth

**Type:** Major city, recurring campaign hub
**Landmarks:** The Conclave, the Button Museum *(formerly)*, [[Rilano (Rolano)|Rilano]]'s fabrial lab, Taravangian's palace
**Regular haunt:** The Glyphpool tavern
**Notable incident:** Arson

## Overview
Kharbranth shows up in this campaign's history often enough to qualify as a second home, which says a lot about a city whose highlights include a burned-down museum, a palace vault heist, and a near-miss with a Shardbearer in the throne room. It's home to the Conclave — the city's library and seat of administration, for anyone who wanted Kharbranth to sound respectable for a paragraph — as well as [[Rilano (Rolano)|Rilano]]'s fabrial lab and Taravangian's palace, plus the Glyphpool tavern, a recurring meeting spot for the party whenever business brought them through.

The city's single most infamous entry on this wiki, though, is the Button Museum — robbed blind and then set ablaze by [[Nucko]] and [[Theren]] in a job that started as reconnaissance in disguise and ended with a stolen artifact rumored to belong to Jezrien himself, roughly two hundred additional buttons, and an entire building on fire on the way out. The full, deeply unflattering account lives on [[Nucko]]'s page, under "The Lia Job," and is worth the read.

Taravangian's palace furnished the rest of Kharbranth's résumé: a vault infiltration where the party turned up a copy of [[The Diagram]], and a considerably tenser confrontation in the throne room itself, where the shardbearer Graves got his hands on [[Theren]] and very nearly didn't let go. He did, eventually — Theren's ending came from somewhere else, later — but it's the kind of close call that earns a city its own file.

## Key Events
- The Button Museum heist and arson.
- Taravangian's vault infiltration; the party discovered a copy of the Diagram.
- Confrontation in Taravangian's throne room; Graves the shardbearer grappled Theren.
