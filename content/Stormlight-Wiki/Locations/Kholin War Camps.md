# Kholin War Camps (Shattered Plains)

## Overview
The party's current home base. Site of their storefront/front business [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)]], the secret lair Kael carved beneath it, multiple [[Church of the Yips]] chapels, and Highprince Aladar's camp (Gavlin's family).

## Key Events
- Establishing the Glyph Pool boutique as a front for the party's operations.
- Nucko's Mega-Reveal of the Ghostblood conspiracy.
- The final showdown against Sebarial took place at a remote research outpost on the Plains nearby.
