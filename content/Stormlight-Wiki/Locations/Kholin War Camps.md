# Kholin War Camps (Shattered Plains)

**Type:** Home base (current tenant status: complicated)
**Located at:** The Shattered Plains
**Landmarks:** [[The Glyph Pool (Shop)]], assorted [[Church of the Yips]] chapels, Highprince Aladar's camp
**Notable residents:** Highprince Aladar's people, including [[Gavlin]]'s family

## Overview
The Kholin War Camps have been the party's home base for most of the campaign — or at least the closest thing a group of career criminals, Radiants, and one very determined button thief has had to a fixed address. It's where the Glyph Pool boutique got set up as a front for the party's actual operations, where [[Nucko]] dropped the single biggest reveal of the campaign by casually confessing to being a lifelong Ghostblood double agent, and, not far off on the Plains, where the whole story eventually came to a head in the final showdown against [[Sebarial]] at a remote research outpost.

The camps are also, technically, still home — Highprince Aladar's people are stationed here, including [[Gavlin]]'s own family, and the multiple chapels of the [[Church of the Yips]] scattered through camp suggest at least one religion has taken hold among the soldiery whether the Highprinces sanctioned it or not. Whether it still counts as "home base" for the whole party is a fairer question than it used to be, given that its most notorious resident has since packed a bag and walked off toward a different fight entirely. See [[Nucko]].

## Key Events
- Establishing the Glyph Pool boutique as a front for the party's operations.
- Nucko's Mega-Reveal of the Ghostblood conspiracy.
