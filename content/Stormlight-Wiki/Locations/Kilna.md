# Kilna

## Overview
A Thaylen port city thrown into revolt, dark-eyes rising up against light-eyes. The party defended the city with an elaborate trap-filled siege against an incoming mercenary army and executed the tyrannical city lord [[Stukov]].

## Key Events
- Interrogation and execution of Stukov.
- Tasni's evacuation of the dark-eyed population to the Reshi Isles.
- The trap-based defense of the city (pits, Kael's traps) against a Shardbearer-led force.
