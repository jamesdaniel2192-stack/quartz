# Kilna

**Type:** Thaylen port city
**Status on arrival:** Mid-revolt, dark-eyes vs. light-eyes
**Status on departure:** Defended, evacuated, one tyrant fewer

## Overview
Kilna was already coming apart when the party showed up — a Thaylen port city where the dark-eyed population had finally had enough of the light-eyed status quo and said so, loudly. The party's answer to an incoming mercenary army was to turn the whole city into a trap: pits, ambush points, and whatever [[Kael]] could rig up with the time available, all deployed against a force marching in under Shardbearer backing. It worked, which is more than can be said for most plans built around "let's just booby-trap an entire city."

Somewhere in the middle of all that, the party interrogated and executed the tyrannical city lord [[Stukov]] — no great loss to anyone, least of all Kilna — while Tasni handled the evacuation of the dark-eyed population out to the Reshi Isles, getting the people the fighting was actually for safely out of the blast radius before things got worse. How exactly [[Sebarial]] threads into all of this, the record doesn't spell out in much detail — the connection's there, filed and cross-referenced, but the specifics apparently didn't make it into anyone's notes.

## Key Events
- Interrogation and execution of Stukov.
- Tasni's evacuation of the dark-eyed population to the Reshi Isles.
- The trap-based defense of the city (pits, Kael's traps) against a Shardbearer-led force.
