# The Glyph Pool (boutique)

## Overview
The party's front business in the [[Kholin War Camps]] — "Gellis & Joran's" premium market for fine threads, boutique buttons, custom footwear, and imported stone sculptures. Built and run by [[Dblar]], with a hidden two-story underground lair carved beneath it by [[Kael]].

## Notable Features
- Hidden entrance beneath a heavy stone rug.
- Custom quarters, an armory for Torin's Shardbow, a study for Ravan, and a dining lounge with a bar.
- A secret stone vault hidden in the lounge wall, kept even from Dblar.
