# The Shallow Crypts

**Type:** Hidden fabrial-research facility, coastal hideout
**Operator:** Captain [[Sebarial]] *(deceased)*
**Built the tools that:** unknowingly enabled an attempt on a god's life
**Current status:** Case closed — see [[Nucko]]

## Overview
The Shallow Crypts was Sebarial's real workshop — a sprawling fabrial-research operation tucked inside a coastal hideout, docked and hidden well away from anywhere a curious Highprince might stumble across it. This is where [[Gavlin]] did some of his best work: a fabrial capable of harvesting raw Investiture straight out of highstorms, and a Stormlight storage system that finally didn't leak. Genuinely brilliant engineering, executed by a kid who had no idea he was building the exact tools his mentor needed to make an attempt on a god's life. Gavlin also hit his Second Ideal here and stepped onto the Enlightened Truthwatcher path — a nice silver lining, or extremely on-the-nose irony, depending on how charitable you're feeling about the whole "unwitting arms supplier" arrangement.

The facility is also where the truth finally surfaced: futuresight and a torn letter signed by someone calling themselves "Cephandrius" laid Sebarial's actual plan bare. Everything downstream of that discovery — up to and including the fight that finally ended him — traces back to what got found in this building.

## Key Events
- Gavlin's Second Ideal breakthrough and Enlightened Truthwatcher path.
- Discovery of Sebarial's true plan via futuresight and a torn letter from "Cephandrius."
- The campaign's climax occurred at a remote research outpost tied to this facility — see [[Kholin War Camps]].
