# The Shallow Crypts

## Overview
Captain Sebarial's sprawling hidden fabrial-research facility, docked directly inside a coastal hideout. Here [[Gavlin]] built a fabrial to harvest raw Investiture from highstorms and a leak-proof Stormlight storage system — unknowingly building the tools Sebarial needed to try to kill a god.

## Key Events
- Gavlin's Second Ideal breakthrough and Enlightened Truthwatcher path.
- Discovery of Sebarial's true plan via futuresight and a torn letter from "Cephandrius."
- The campaign's climax occurred at a remote research outpost tied to this facility.
