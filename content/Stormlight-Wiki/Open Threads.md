# Open Threads & Mysteries

Running list of unresolved plot lines as of the most recent session (8/17/26 — Kael's death and the fall of Sebarial).

- **The Book of the Returned forgery** — will [[Sebarial]] (or his associates) ever discover the swapped/falsified text pointing him toward Shinovar?
- **Halidar & Melania** — the two remaining Ghostbloods who killed [[Nucko]]'s parents are still unaccounted for.
- **Vesper's hidden informant** — who holds the identities of Nucko's parents' killers, still stashed somewhere in the War Camps.
- **[[Torin]] & [[Liss]]** — relationship status: officially courting.
- **[[Jaro]]'s loyalties** — last seen actively assisting Sebarial; where do his allegiances lie now that Sebarial is dead?
- **Kael's death** — what happens to the party (and to Cadenza) now that a Third Ideal Radiant has passed into the Beyond?
- **The Everstorm** — was actively approaching; full consequences unclear.
- **Dahl / Nico** — the twin impersonation scheme within House Renmuri, and Dahl's ultimate goals for [[The Diagram]], remain unresolved.
- **Odium's Perpendicularity** — its real location on the Shattered Plains is still a live thread, now that Sebarial (who was hunting it) is gone.
