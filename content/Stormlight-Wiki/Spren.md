# Spren

Five bonds, one severed, and a running theme of spren who clearly knew what they were signing up for before their humans did. This is the roster.

| Spren | Bonded To | Order | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cadenza | [[Kael]] | Willshaper | Was already following Kael around since his circus days, silently, for who knows how long, before finally deciding to say something. |
| Masken | [[Nucko]] | Lightweaver-type | The only entity in this campaign who has managed to talk Nucko through anything resembling his feelings about fire. Actual therapy, apparently, works. |
| Glimmer in Doubt | [[Gavlin]] | Truthwatcher ("Enlightened") | Touched by Odium via Sja-Anat and grants futuresight — which is to say, Gavlin got the one spren upgrade that comes with an asterisk the size of a building. |
| Twoie | [[Ravan (Amanrava)]] | Skybreaker | Locked in a running debate with Ravan about justice and morality that shows no sign of ever resolving, because that's sort of the whole job description. |
| Geoffrey | [[Theren]] *(bond broken)* | — | Severed the day Theren embraced the Thrill and became its Champion instead. This is the entry the wiki wishes it didn't have to write. |
